## ✨ Welcome

This is the open source home of the **Noor team**.

Here we host the team's open source projects for the community to **use, explore, contribute to, and collaborate on**. Our goal is to build useful tools in the open and make it easy for developers, contributors, and curious builders to take part.

## 🤝 What you'll find here

- Open source projects created and maintained by the Noor team
- Repositories the community can use in their own work
- Opportunities to contribute, improve, and collaborate
- A shared space for transparent, community-driven development

## 🚀 Collaborate with us

We believe open source is better when it is built together.

If you would like to collaborate, you can:

- Explore our repositories
- Open issues to report bugs or suggest ideas
- Submit pull requests with improvements
- Join discussions and help shape future work

## 💙 Built for the community

Everything hosted here is intended to support the wider community and encourage open collaboration around the Noor team's work.

Whether you're here to use a project, contribute code, or share feedback — **welcome**.
